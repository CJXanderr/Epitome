Regular Expressions

With Search → Enable Regex (⌥⌘R) the whole search box is one pattern, matched against names (or full paths with Match Path). The regex: modifier makes a single term a pattern while the rest of the search uses normal syntax.

PatternMatches
a|ba or b
gr(a|e)ygray or grey
.any character
[abc]a, b or c
[^abc]anything but a, b or c
[a-z]a range
\d \w \sa digit, a word character, white space
^start of the name
$end of the name
*previous item 0 or more times
+1 or more times
?0 or 1 time
{3}exactly 3 times
{3,}3 or more times
{3,5}3 to 5 times
\.a literal dot (escape special characters with \)
(?i) (?-i)turn case-insensitivity on / off inside the pattern

Examples

SearchFinds
regex:^IMG_\d{4}\.(jpe?g|heic)$camera photos
regex:[^\x00-\x7f]names with non-ASCII characters
regex:"\(\d+\)\."Finder-style copies such as “file (2).pdf”
regex:^\d{4}-\d{2}-\d{2}names starting with a date
path:regex:/node_modules/.*\.md$Markdown files inside node_modules folders

Quote a regex term that contains spaces or | so the search parser doesn’t split it. Patterns ignore case unless Match Case or case: is on. Epitome uses the ICU regular-expression engine.
