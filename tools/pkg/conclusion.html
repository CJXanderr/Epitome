Epitome is installed.

  1. Open Epitome from your Applications folder. The first scan of your disk takes under a minute; after that it starts instantly.
  2. For a complete index, open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access and turn on Epitome. Without it, protected folders (Mail, Messages, other apps’ data) are skipped.
  3. Optional: set a system-wide hotkey in Epitome → Settings → Keyboard to bring up Epitome from any app.

In Terminal, try ep -help while Epitome is running.
